# Parents Guide

A short guide for parents: how Math Heroes measures progress and how to start over.

## Difficulty levels

Your child picks one difficulty level. The choice is saved in the browser.

- **Easy:** numbers up to 10
- **Medium:** numbers up to 30
- **Hard:** numbers up to 50

## How stars are earned

- Each level has 5 questions.
- Score per level:
  - 5 correct: 3 stars
  - 4 correct: 2 stars
  - 3 correct: 1 star
  - Fewer than 3 correct: 0 stars
- Each level shows up to 3 stars on the map.
- A level keeps its best result. A new score only replaces the old one if it is higher.
- Each world has 5 levels, so one world gives up to 15 stars. All four worlds give up to 60 stars. The map shows this as "Total stars: N of 60".

## How worlds and levels unlock

- **Speedy Zone** (Sonic) and **Mushroom Kingdom** (Mario) are open from the start.
- Inside Speedy Zone and Mushroom Kingdom, each level opens after the level before it has at least 1 star.
- **Web City** (Spidey) opens after 4 stars in total from Sonic and Mario together.
- Inside Web City, each level opens after the level before it has at least 1 star.
- **Hallownest** (Hollow Knight) opens after 4 stars in total in Web City.
- Inside Hallownest, each level opens after the level before it has at least 1 star.

## How to reset progress

- Press the **Reset Stars** button on the map. The game asks you to confirm first.
- Confirming sets all stars to 0. This locks Web City and Hallownest again, and every level after the first one in Speedy Zone, Mushroom Kingdom, Web City and Hallownest.
- Reset does not change the chosen difficulty or the daily score records.
- For a full clean start, clear this website's saved data in the browser. The game saves to two keys: `mathHeroes_v1` (stars and difficulty) and `mathHeroesRecords_v1` (daily scores). Clearing site data removes both.
