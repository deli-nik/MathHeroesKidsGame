# The Four Worlds

Math Heroes has four worlds. Each world has 5 levels, and each level has 5 questions. A level earns 0 to 3 stars, based on how many questions the child answers correctly on the first try.

## Speedy Zone

| Hero | Skill | Collectible |
|---|---|---|
| Sonic | Addition | Rings |

- **Levels:** 5. Level 1 is open from the start.
- **Stars:** 5 first-try correct = 3 stars, 4 = 2 stars, 3 = 1 star, 2 or fewer = 0 stars.
- **Friends:** Tails flies in and adds rings. Knuckles digs up rings. Both are helpers.
- **How it unlocks:** Open from the start.

## Mushroom Kingdom

| Hero | Skill | Collectible |
|---|---|---|
| Mario | Subtraction | Coins |

- **Levels:** 5. Level 1 is open from the start.
- **Stars:** 5 first-try correct = 3 stars, 4 = 2 stars, 3 = 1 star, 2 or fewer = 0 stars.
- **Friends and enemies:** There are no helper friends in this world. Mario spends coins on a power-up. A sneaky Goomba takes coins. Yoshi gobbles coins. The Goomba and Yoshi are not helpers.
- **How it unlocks:** Open from the start.

## Web City

| Hero | Skill | Collectible |
|---|---|---|
| Spidey | Add & subtract | Balloons |

- **Levels:** 5.
- **Stars:** 5 first-try correct = 3 stars, 4 = 2 stars, 3 = 1 star, 2 or fewer = 0 stars.
- **Friends:** There are no friends in this world.
- **Adding:** Spidey catches balloons, webs them, or they float up from the street.
- **Taking away:** Balloons float away, burst, or Spidey gives them to kids below.
- **How it unlocks:** Earn 4 stars in total across Sonic's and Mario's levels together (all 10 levels).

## Hallownest

| Hero | Skill | Collectible |
|---|---|---|
| Hollow Knight | Add & subtract | Geos |

- **Levels:** 5.
- **Stars:** 5 first-try correct = 3 stars, 4 = 2 stars, 3 = 1 star, 2 or fewer = 0 stars.
- **Friends and enemies:**
  - Adding: Hornet tosses geos (a helper). A happy Grub gives geos (a helper). A beetle drops geos when the Knight slashes it.
  - Taking away: The Knight spends geos at Sly's shop. A Tiktik knocks geos away (an enemy). The Knight gives geos to the little Grubs.
- **How it unlocks:** Earn 4 stars in total across Web City's 5 levels.

## Level unlocks (every world)

Level 1 is open in Speedy Zone and Mushroom Kingdom from the start. In every world, each later level (levels 2 to 5) unlocks when the level before it has at least 1 star.

## Totals and resetting

- There are 60 stars in total across all 20 levels (4 worlds × 5 levels × 3 stars).
- Resetting stars from the map locks Web City and Hallownest again.
